FAQ

Is it safe? Why does Windows warn me?

Windows shows that warning for every app that isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate. ClipDeck is a small project for friends, so it isn't. It needs no admin rights and installs only for your user.

Does it slow down my games?

Hardly. The video is encoded on your graphics card, and ClipDeck itself typically uses around 1 % of the CPU while it keeps your last seconds ready.

Does it upload anything?

No. Everything stays on your PC. ClipDeck only goes online to check for updates, and to send a clip to Discord if you ask it to.

How do I update?

You don't have to. ClipDeck downloads new versions by itself and installs them the next time it starts.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → ClipDeck → Uninstall. Your clips stay in Videos\ClipDeck.

Something doesn't work

Open ClipDeck → Settings → About → Logs and send the newest file to whoever shared ClipDeck with you.
